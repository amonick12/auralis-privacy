Auralis Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026 App: Auralis (Auralis: Sound & Presence), iOS Developer: Aaron Monick ("we", "us") Contact: amonick12@gmail.com

Auralis is an adaptive audio companion. You speak, music plays, and Auralis listens and responds when it would help. We designed it so that your words, sessions and journal stay on your iPhone.

The short version

  • No account. Auralis doesn't ask who you are.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. We don't collect usage data and don't use third-party SDKs.
  • No audio is ever recorded, stored or sent. The microphone is used for live, on-device transcription during a session, and, if you keep music from another app playing, to recognize the song.
  • Your sessions and journal are stored on your device, and in your own private iCloud only if you turn on iCloud sync.
  • Your words leave your device only if you add your own key for an external AI provider, and then only to that provider, for the feature you're using.

What Auralis stores on your device

Stored locally on your iPhone, not on our servers (we have none):

  • Sessions: transcripts of what you said, Auralis's replies, bookmarks, the music played, summaries and themes.
  • Journal: entries you write, import or add after sessions.
  • Preferences: settings such as voice, voice boost and talkativeness.
  • Music preferences: which kinds of music you skipped or listened through, used to choose better music.
  • Journal images: pictures you ask Auralis to make for an entry.
  • Recognized songs you choose to save.
  • Spoken audio cache: if you use an OpenAI voice, the generated speech is kept on your device so the same line isn't generated twice.
  • Technical logs: audio, voice and song-recognition states (for example, "music resumed"), kept on your device. They never contain what was said.
  • Your AI API keys, if you add any: stored only in the iOS Keychain, never in the app's database or logs.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default). If you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, your sessions, journal (including pictures) and settings are also kept in your private iCloud database, so they match across your devices, and your AI keys sync through iCloud Keychain. This uses your Apple Account under Apple's terms; we can't see it. iCloud Keychain is end-to-end encrypted; your sessions and journal are too if you turn on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud. Turning sync off keeps everything on the device.

App lock (optional, off by default). You can lock Auralis with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode in Settings → Privacy. The check is done by iOS; Auralis never sees your face or passcode. When the lock is on, the app is covered in the app switcher, your journal is removed from Spotlight, and Siri won't read it.

You can delete individual sessions (History) and journal entries (Journal), delete all journal entries (Settings → Journal), or delete everything by deleting the app. Like other apps, this data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, under Apple's terms.

Microphone and speech

When a session is running and listening is on, Auralis uses the microphone to transcribe your speech on your device, using Apple's speech recognition. Audio is processed in memory and discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn listening off at any time during a session.

Song recognition

If you keep music from another app playing during a session (for example YouTube or Spotify), Auralis can recognize the song using Apple's ShazamKit. A few seconds of microphone audio are turned on your device into a signature (a fingerprint of the music that can't be turned back into sound or speech), and only that signature is sent to Apple's Shazam service to find the song. Auralis doesn't listen for songs while it is speaking. Recognition is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Artificial intelligence

Auralis generates replies in one of these ways, depending on your settings:

  1. On-device (Apple Intelligence): on supported iPhones, replies are generated on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  2. Private Cloud Compute (optional, off by default): if you enable it, requests may be processed by Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designed so your data is not retained or accessible to Apple.
  3. External AI provider (optional): if you add your own API key for a provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or a server you set up), the text of the current conversation, and, in Journaling, Custom, Dream Exploration and Active Imagination sessions, a few passages from your past sessions or journal that are relevant to what you just said, is sent to that provider to generate a reply. If you've added keys for more than one and the one you chose is unavailable, Auralis may use another one you added. When you ask a question about your past sessions (in Search, or by voice), the question and the passages that match it are sent to check the matches and write the answer. When you ask Paths to make a path from your journal, the theme you chose and up to six entries about it are sent to write the days. The title and artist of the song playing are included so replies can refer to it. In the Journal, features you start yourself send the entries they work on: reflecting on an entry sends that entry and up to three earlier ones; a symbol's thread sends the entries that mention it; Looking Back sends every entry in the span you chose (a week, a month or three months); adding a session to your journal sends its notes to be rewritten in your voice; proofreading an entry sends that entry. To tell dreams from other entries, Auralis also sends the opening lines of each entry once. That data is handled under the provider's own terms and privacy policy. Auralis never sends audio.
  4. Built-in responses: without any of the above, Auralis uses fixed, on-device responses and sends nothing.

OpenAI voices and journal images (optional)

If you add an OpenAI API key and choose an OpenAI voice, the text Auralis is about to speak is sent to OpenAI to turn it into speech: Auralis's own replies and guidance, and any text you ask it to read aloud, such as a journal entry or text you share to Auralis from another app. Your voice is never sent.

If you ask Auralis to make an image for a journal entry, the entry's text is sent to your AI provider (above) to describe a scene, and only that scene description is sent to OpenAI to paint the picture. The image is stored with the entry on your device.

Both are handled under OpenAI's terms and privacy policy.

Beta feedback (TestFlight only)

In test builds, Auralis may ask after a session how it went. If you choose to send feedback, it goes by email from your own Mail app (or the share sheet), and you see it before it's sent. It contains your note, the app build and device model, and, only if you leave Include the transcript and logs on, that session's setup, transcript, timeline and technical logs. We use it only to fix problems, and delete it on request. The App Store version doesn't ask.

Apple Health (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Log to Apple Health in Settings, Auralis writes two kinds of entries to the Health app on your iPhone: Mindful Minutes for finished Meditation, Breathwork, Shamanic Journey, Album Journey and Inquiry sessions, and State of Mind from your arriving and leaving check-ins.

Separately, only if you turn on Show sleep and heart patterns, Auralis reads your sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and the mindful minutes other apps logged, to show patterns with your practice in Paths and Noticing (for example, how you slept on nights after a session). This is read on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere: not to an AI provider, not to iCloud, not to us. Nothing about Health leaves your device. You can revoke or change either at any time in the Health app or in Auralis's settings.

Music

If you connect Apple Music, Auralis uses Apple's MusicKit to play music and read your library or playlists for soundtrack choices. Playback and your Apple Music account are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Auralis does not share your sessions or journal with Apple Music. If you keep music from another app playing, Auralis reads only what the system reports as playing (title and artist), and can pause or resume it when you ask.

Safety features

In some modes, Auralis can offer to call a trusted contact you entered, or emergency services. Nothing is ever dialed or sent without you tapping to place the call, and your trusted contact is stored only on your device. Auralis is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Children

Auralis is not directed to children under 13. We don't collect personal data, apart from beta feedback a tester chooses to send.
