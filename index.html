Auralis Privacy Policy

Effective: October 8, 2026 App: Auralis (Auralis: Sound & Presence), iOS Developer: Aaron Monick ("we", "us") Contact: amonick12@gmail.com

Auralis is an adaptive audio companion. You speak, music plays, and Auralis listens and responds when it would help. We designed it so that your words, sessions and journal stay on your iPhone.

The short version

  • No account. Auralis doesn't ask who you are.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. We don't collect usage data and don't use third-party SDKs.
  • No audio is ever recorded, stored or sent. The microphone is used for live, on-device transcription during a session, and, if you keep music from another app playing, to recognize the song.
  • Your sessions and journal are stored on your device, and in your own private iCloud only if you turn on iCloud sync.
  • Your words leave your device only if you add your own key for an external AI provider, and then only to that provider, for the feature you're using.

What Auralis stores on your device

Stored locally on your iPhone, not on our servers (we have none):

  • Sessions: transcripts of what you said, Auralis's replies, bookmarks, the music played, summaries and themes.
  • Journal: entries you write, import or add after sessions.
  • Preferences: settings such as voice, voice boost and talkativeness.
  • Music preferences: which kinds of music you skipped or listened through, used to choose better music.
  • Journal images: pictures you ask Auralis to make for an entry.
  • Recognized songs you choose to save.
  • Spoken audio cache: if you use an OpenAI voice, the generated speech is kept on your device so the same line isn't generated twice.
  • Technical logs: audio, voice and song-recognition states (for example, "music resumed"), kept on your device. They never contain what was said.
  • Your AI API keys, if you add any: stored only in the iOS Keychain, never in the app's database or logs.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default). If you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, your sessions, journal (including pictures) and settings are also kept in your private iCloud database, so they match across your devices, and your AI keys sync through iCloud Keychain. This uses your Apple Account under Apple's terms; we can't see it. iCloud Keychain is end-to-end encrypted; your sessions and journal are too if you turn on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud. Turning sync off keeps everything on the device.

App lock (optional, off by default). You can lock Auralis with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode in Settings → Privacy. The check is done by iOS; Auralis never sees your face or passcode. When the lock is on, the app is covered in the app switcher, your journal is removed from Spotlight, and Siri won't read it.

You can delete individual sessions (History) and journal entries (Journal), delete all journal entries (Settings → Journal), or delete everything by deleting the app. Like other apps, this data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, under Apple's terms.

Microphone and speech

When a session is running and listening is on, Auralis uses the microphone to transcribe your speech on your device, using Apple's speech recognition. Audio is processed in memory and discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn listening off at any time during a session.

Song recognition

If you keep music from another app playing during a session (for example YouTube or Spotify), Auralis can recognize the song using Apple's ShazamKit. A few seconds of microphone audio are turned on your device into a signature (a fingerprint of the music that can't be turned back into sound or speech), and only that signature is sent to Apple's Shazam service to find the song. Auralis doesn't listen for songs while it is speaking. Recognition is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Artificial intelligence

Auralis generates replies in one of these ways, depending on your settings:

  1. On-device (Apple Intelligence): on supported iPhones, replies are generated on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. On devices without Apple Intelligence, you can choose in Settings to download a private open model (Qwen3 1.7B or 4B on a phone, about 1 to 2.3 GB, from Hugging Face). The download contacts Hugging Face's servers for the model files only; no personal data is sent. Once downloaded, replies are generated on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. You can remove the model in Settings. Your Mac: if you turn on sharing in Auralis on your Mac, your iPhone and iPad can use the Mac's larger downloaded model, at home or away. The devices connect directly to each other over an encrypted peer-to-peer connection (WebRTC); requests and replies go only between your devices. To set up the connection, each device writes its network addresses and a signed offer or answer to your private iCloud database, and asks public STUN servers (Google, Cloudflare) for its own public address; no conversation text passes through iCloud or any server. A shared secret in iCloud Keychain makes sure only your own devices can connect. On the Mac, a larger model can run in Auralis Model Host, a companion Mac app, which shares it with your iPhone and iPad the same way (your private iCloud for setup, the same iCloud Keychain secret, a direct encrypted connection); Auralis on the Mac can also pass it requests over the Mac's own loopback address, which nothing outside the Mac can reach.
  2. Private Cloud Compute: not used. Auralis will offer it, off by default, once Apple approves it for the app; then requests may be processed by Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designed so your data is not retained or accessible to Apple.
  3. External AI provider (optional): if you add your own API key for a provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or a server you set up), the text of the current conversation, and, in Journaling, Custom, Record a Dream, Dreamwork, Active Imagination and Arcana Reading sessions, a few passages from your past sessions or journal that are relevant to what you just said, is sent to that provider to generate a reply. If you've added keys for more than one and the one you chose is unavailable, Auralis may use another one you added. If you've turned on any traditions (Settings → Traditions), their names and a sentence about each go with the request (replies, Looking Back, the briefing for readings), so they can use their terms. Tradition reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing about them is sent anywhere. When you ask a question about your past sessions (in Search, or by voice), the question and the passages that match it are sent to check the matches and write the answer. When you ask Paths to make a path from your journal, the theme you chose and up to six entries about it are sent to write the days. The title and artist of the song playing are included so replies can refer to it. In the Journal, features you start yourself send the entries they work on: reflecting on an entry sends that entry and up to three earlier ones; a symbol's thread sends the entries that mention it; Looking Back sends every entry in the span you chose (a week, a month or three months); adding a session to your journal sends its notes to be rewritten in your voice; proofreading an entry sends that entry. To tell dreams from other entries, Auralis also sends the opening lines of each entry once. That data is handled under the provider's own terms and privacy policy. In Storytelling and Learning, the topic you give is sent too; if you choose to continue a dream as a story, that dream's text is sent as the topic. In Dreamwork, the dream you choose is sent so the replies can work with it. Auralis never sends audio.
  4. Built-in responses: without any of the above, Auralis uses fixed, on-device responses and sends nothing.

Which one answers. With AI Provider set to Private, only the on-device models (1) answer, and nothing goes to a service. With External, only your provider (3) answers. With Automatic, Auralis uses the best available: your provider (3) first, if you've added a key and are online, then your Mac's model, then Apple's on-device model, then this device's downloaded model, then built-in responses. So with Automatic and a key, conversations are sent to your provider whenever you're online; choose Private to keep them on your devices.

Photos of tarot cards (Arcana Reading) If you photograph your cards or choose a photo of them for Arcana Reading, Auralis reads the card names on your device with Apple's text recognition and keeps only which cards you drew. Your photo is compared with the reference cards on your device; it isn't stored or sent anywhere. The cards, their traditional meanings and your question are part of the reading, sent to an AI provider only as described above. So the reading can be about your life, it also includes, while "Journal informs sessions" is on in Settings: a few lines from each of your last several journal entries and recent dreams (from the past three weeks), your journal's recurring themes this month, your last session's summary and your path day, and a private portrait of your life (the same, without the portrait, when you ask setup to suggest questions): a briefing on the experiences, people, places, ideas and dreams in your journal, which a model writes from your whole journal when you first set up a reading and then updates with your new entries. The portrait is kept on your device. Writing or updating it sends your journal (or its new entries) to the model doing the writing: your AI provider's strongest model if you've added a key and chose Automatic or External, otherwise your Mac's model. It is never written by a model on this device, which isn't accurate enough for it. A second pass by the same model checks the portrait against your journal, line by line, and removes anything the journal doesn't support (so your journal goes to that model twice). Each line quotes the words of the entry it comes from; your device then drops any line whose words aren't found in your journal (this check stays on the device). The same goes for Looking Back's letters, the threads of a symbol or a dream series, reading your dreams for series, and writing a personal path. Without a key or your Mac, those wait until one is available.

The Library (an optional tab) keeps its courses and your progress on your device, synced through iCloud with your settings when sync is on. When you add a topic, what you typed and the names of the traditions you've turned on go to the same model (the strongest hosted model with your key, else your Mac's) to write its course. "Suggest more for me" sends your traditions and the themes of your sessions and journal (single words, not your entries). Going deeper into a finished course, writing a tradition's course afresh, or writing a part out to read sends the course's outline (not your journal) to that model. A Library course or story is taught in a Learning or Storytelling session like any other. Asking about a lesson, recall questions and fact-checking a written course send that course's text (not your journal) to the same model. "Where this shows up for you" sends a few journal entries that touch the part to your own Mac's model only, never a hosted one. A topic or path without an icon of its own gets one drawn by OpenAI's image model when you've added an OpenAI key: its title and a line about it are sent, nothing else. "A lesson each morning" is a notification scheduled on your device with a line from a course you follow; nothing is sent anywhere. Sharing a topic makes a link you send yourself: a built-in topic's link carries only its name, and one written for you carries its course outline, glossary, texts and stories (not your progress, your journal or the lessons written out for you).

The same portrait (shortened for a model on your own devices) also goes with each request in conversation sessions (Journaling, Dream sessions, Active Imagination, Inquiry, Custom, and Psychedelic Support when exploring ideas), so Auralis understands who you are; with a key, starting one of those sessions may update it in the background. Turn "Journal informs sessions" off to keep all of this out.

OpenAI voices and journal images (optional)

If you add an OpenAI API key and choose an OpenAI voice, the text Auralis is about to speak is sent to OpenAI to turn it into speech: Auralis's own replies and guidance, and any text you ask it to read aloud, such as a journal entry or text you share to Auralis from another app. Your voice is never sent.

If you ask Auralis to make an image for a journal entry, the entry's text is sent to your AI provider (above) to describe a scene, and only that scene description is sent to OpenAI to paint the picture. The image is stored with the entry on your device.

Both are handled under OpenAI's terms and privacy policy.

Beta feedback (TestFlight only)

In test builds, Auralis may ask after a session how it went. If you choose to send feedback, it goes by email from your own Mail app (or the share sheet), and you see it before it's sent. It contains your note, the app build and device model, and, only if you leave Include the transcript and logs on, that session's setup, transcript, timeline and technical logs. We use it only to fix problems, and delete it on request. The App Store version doesn't ask.

Apple Health (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Log to Apple Health in Settings, Auralis writes two kinds of entries to the Health app on your iPhone: Mindful Minutes for finished Meditation, Breathwork, Shamanic Journey, Album Journey and Inquiry sessions, and State of Mind from your arriving and leaving check-ins.

Separately, only if you turn on Show sleep and heart patterns, Auralis reads your sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and the mindful minutes other apps logged, to show patterns with your practice in Paths and Noticing (for example, how you slept on nights after a session). This is read on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere: not to an AI provider, not to iCloud, not to us. Nothing about Health leaves your device. You can revoke or change either at any time in the Health app or in Auralis's settings.

After a Sleep session, the session shows how long it took you to fall asleep: Auralis asks once (with Apple's Health sheet) to read sleep, and finds when the night's sleep began after the session started. This is read on your iPhone in the same way, kept only on your iPhone (not in iCloud), and never leaves it.

Apple Watch. If you have the Auralis Watch app, it follows a session started on your iPhone: it runs a mindfulness workout to read your heart rate, shows it, and sends it to the iPhone, where the session can ease the music toward calm when your heart rate climbs. The workout isn't saved to Health. The heart rate is noted in that session's log about once a minute, so it's stored with the session (on your device, and in your private iCloud if you turn on sync). It's never sent to an AI provider. You can turn the Watch off for sessions, or just the music easing, in Settings → Health & Devices.

Music

If you connect Apple Music, Auralis uses Apple's MusicKit to play music and read your library or playlists for soundtrack choices. Playback and your Apple Music account are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Auralis does not share your sessions or journal with Apple Music. If you keep music from another app playing, Auralis reads only what the system reports as playing (title and artist), and can pause or resume it when you ask.

Safety features

In some modes, Auralis can offer to call a trusted contact you entered, or emergency services. Nothing is ever dialed or sent without you tapping to place the call, and your trusted contact is stored only on your device. Auralis is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Children

Auralis is not directed to children under 13. We don't collect personal data, apart from beta feedback a tester chooses to send.
